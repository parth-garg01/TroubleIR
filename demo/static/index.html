SIIS Article — Battery drain
If your Galaxy device battery is draining faster than expected, there are several settings you can adjust. First, check Battery Usage in Settings to identify which apps are consuming the most power. Navigate to Settings > Battery and device care > Battery > Battery usage. Apps running in the background can significantly drain battery. Go to Settings > Battery and device care > Battery > Background usage limits and restrict apps that you don't need running in the background. Reduce screen brightness by going to Settings > Display > Brightness and lowering the slider. Enable Power saving mode via Settings > Battery and device care > Battery > Power saving to limit CPU performance and background network activity.
